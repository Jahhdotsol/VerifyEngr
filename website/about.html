About verifyEngr

The problem

Building collapses in Nigeria are often linked to unqualified or unregistered practitioners. Yet most homeowners have no quick way to check that the "engineer" they are paying is really registered with COREN, the Council for the Regulation of Engineering in Nigeria.

Our mission

Make checking an engineer as easy as a quick search, so every building starts with a qualified professional.

What you can do

  • Verify a COREN number in seconds.
  • Search engineers by name, discipline or state.
  • See experience, past projects and registration status.
  • Contact registered engineers, share a result, or report a problem.

Our data

This demo uses sample data modelled on COREN and NSE (Nigerian Society of Engineers) records. No real person's registration is shown. We plan to partner with COREN and NSE to bring in real, up-to-date data.

What's next

  • Official data through a COREN and NSE partnership
  • Consultants and construction companies
  • Engineers claiming and updating their own profiles

verifyEngr does not replace official checks. Always confirm registration with COREN before hiring.
