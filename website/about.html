verifyEngr makes checking an engineer as quick as a search, so homeowners, developers and inspectors know who they are hiring.

The problem

The Building Collapse Prevention Guild has recorded 679 building collapses and more than 1,639 deaths in Nigeria since 1974 (Nigeria Info, November 2025). Experts point to substandard materials, weak enforcement and unqualified people posing as professionals.

Most people hiring for a building project have no quick way to check that their engineer is registered with COREN, the Council for the Regulation of Engineering in Nigeria.

What you can do here

  • Verify a COREN number in seconds.
  • Search engineers by name, discipline or state.
  • See experience, past projects and registration status on one record.
  • Email a registered engineer, share a record on WhatsApp, or report a problem.

Our data

This demo uses sample data modelled on COREN and NSE (Nigerian Society of Engineers) records. No real person's registration is shown. We plan to partner with COREN and NSE to bring in real, up-to-date records.

What comes next

  • Official records through a COREN and NSE partnership
  • Consultants and construction companies
  • Engineers claiming and updating their own records

verifyEngr does not replace official checks. Always confirm registration with COREN before hiring. Read our privacy and disclaimer.
