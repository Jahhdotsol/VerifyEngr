Privacy and disclaimer

What verifyEngr shows, what it does not do, and how your information is handled. Last updated 17 November 2026.

This is a demo with sample data

Every engineer on verifyEngr today is sample data. The names, COREN numbers (all starting with "SAMPLE-"), emails and projects are made up to show how the service works. They are not real COREN records and do not describe real people.

Not an official check

verifyEngr is an independent project. It is not run by, endorsed by or connected to COREN (the Council for the Regulation of Engineering in Nigeria) or the NSE (Nigerian Society of Engineers).

A verification record on this site is not an official COREN certificate. Before you hire an engineer or start work, always confirm their registration directly with COREN.

"Not found" means only that a number or name is not in our records. It does not prove that a person is unregistered or acting dishonestly.

What we collect

  • We do not ask you to sign up or log in.
  • What you type in the search or verify boxes stays in your browser. It is not sent to us or stored.
  • We do not use cookies, advertising or tracking tools.
  • The site is hosted on Vercel, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses and the pages requested) to keep the service running.

Reports and emails

When you use "Report a problem", your own email app opens a message to momoh_elijah@yahoo.com. We only see what you choose to send, and use it to review and correct records. We do not share reports with anyone else.

"Email this engineer" opens your email app with the engineer's listed address. That conversation is between you and the engineer.

Engineers' information

When real records are added, verifyEngr will show only professional details: name, COREN number, discipline, experience, location, employer and projects. Contact details will appear only with the engineer's agreement.

Corrections

If anything on a record is wrong, use "Report a problem" on that record or email momoh_elijah@yahoo.com.

In short: use verifyEngr to check quickly, then confirm with COREN before anyone starts building.
